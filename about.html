About Us

KADIYAM PLANTS GALLERY

"Raising greenery that connects spaces and uplifts human lives with natural grace."

Rooted in the famous horticultural town of Kadiyam, Andhra Pradesh, Kadiyam Plants Gallery has grown to become a premium supplier of bulk ornamental, fruit, avenue, and decorative flora. For over ten years, we have worked alongside garden designers, villa owners, and corporate developers to provide healthy foliage and execute state-of-the-art landscaping architectures.

Whether you are a home gardener wanting customized flowering border plants or a contractor requiring massive avenue shade trees, we guarantee top-grade soil mixtures, premium packaging, safe transit, and reliable landscaping executions.
