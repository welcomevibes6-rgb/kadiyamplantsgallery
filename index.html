About Us

About Kadiyam Plants Gallery

Kadiyam Plants Gallery, located in Kadiapulanka, Kadiyam Mandal, Andhra Pradesh, is a trusted name in horticulture known for quality and excellence. With over 10 years of experience, we are one of the well-established nurseries in the Kadiyam region, widely recognized for rich greenery and plant diversity.

Our Specializations

  • Avenue Canopy Trees & Shade Plants
  • Topiary & Ficus Standard Crafts
  • Villa & Hotel Landscaping Projects
  • Bulk Supply Across India

"We don't just grow plants — we grow trust, greenery, and happiness."
